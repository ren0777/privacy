Privacy Policy for AnimeTab

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Overview

AnimeTab replaces your new tab page with anime wallpapers, a live clock, a search bar, and daily quotes. It does not require an account and has no backend service of its own.

Data Collection

AnimeTab does not collect personal information, browsing history, or usage analytics. It contains no analytics SDKs, advertising, or tracking scripts.

What Is Stored Locally

AnimeTab uses chrome.storage.local and IndexedDB on your device to remember:

  • nekosWallpaperUrl, anime_bg_url: the address of the current wallpaper.
  • animeThemeMode: your selected theme.
  • customBackgroundEnabled, customBackgroundMime, customBackgroundRef, customBackgroundData: your own uploaded background, if you choose to add one. The file itself stays in your browser.

This data never leaves your Chrome profile.

Network Requests

  • Wallpapers are requested from the public nekos.best API. No personal data is sent with these requests.
  • When you submit a search, you are redirected to Google Search with your query.
  • Shortcut links (e.g. Crunchyroll, MyAnimeList) open those websites only when you click them.

Data Sharing

AnimeTab does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

Data Removal

Stored preferences remain in your browser until you clear extension data or uninstall AnimeTab, which removes them.
